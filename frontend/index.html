Signal runs a small, cheap load test against your application, then fits the Universal Scalability Law, Little's Law, and queueing theory to what it measured — extrapolating throughput, response time, and breaking point far beyond what was actually tested.

      Estimates component-level throughput and service demand using
      Xi = X·Vi and Di = Vi·Si.

      and external APIs where OS-level utilization may not be directly available.
